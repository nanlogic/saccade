# Historical Agent evidence policy

- R-001 — Benchmark and dogfood reports are evidence scoped to their recorded candidate, client, browser, task, route, and date.
- R-002 — A local fixture or historical public-site result does not authorize a general compatibility, speed, token, or release claim.
- R-003 — Conflicting pass and blocker reports remain historical until a current frozen matrix supersedes them explicitly.
- R-004 — Current public claims require complete machine-readable evidence from the frozen release candidate.
